## Calculator Sections

### Home Charging ROI
**Intro**: Installing a Level 2 home charger is the fastest way to charge overnight. But how long until it pays for itself? Our calculator shows your exact payback period and 5-year savings.

**Educational content**: 
- Average Level 2 charger costs $500-$1,200 (equipment)
- Installation typically $500-$2,000 (depends on electrical panel distance)
- Average EV efficiency: 3-4 mi/kWh (Tesla Model 3: 4.1, F-150 Lightning: 2.1)
- National average electricity: $0.14/kWh (varies by state)

**Affiliate opportunities**: Amazon links for NEMA 14-50 outlets, ChargePoint Home, Tesla Wall Connector, JuiceBox

### EV vs Gas TCO
**Intro**: Electric vehicles cost more upfront but save thousands over time. See the complete picture: purchase price, fuel, maintenance, insurance, and incentives.

**Educational content**:
- Federal tax credit: up to $7,500 (income limits apply)
- State incentives vary ($0-$7,500 additional)
- EV maintenance: ~$0.06/mile vs gas $0.10/mile (no oil changes, less brake wear)
- Insurance: EVs typically 10-20% higher than equivalent gas

**Affiliate opportunities**: Links to Carvana, Vroom, AutoTrader (EV listings), insurance comparison (NerdWallet affiliate)

### Quick Savings
**Intro**: The 30-second answer. How much will you save on fuel each year?
